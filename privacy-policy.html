Privacy Policy for HomePro Family Tracker

Last updated: September 28, 2026 • Effective immediately

Welcome to HomePro Family Tracker & Safety ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of your family's personal and location data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android application and associated services.

Prominent Notice on Background Location Access

HomePro is a dedicated family safety, GPS locator, and emergency distress platform. To provide real-time location sharing, automatic geofence safe place alerts (e.g. arrival/departure at School or Home), and 1-tap Emergency SOS broadcasting, this application collects precise location data in both the foreground and background ("all the time"), even when the application is closed or not in active use.

1 Information We Collect

  • Precise GPS Location Data: Latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy, movement speed (km/h), heading, and timestamps required to display your position on your private family circle map and execute geofence transitions.
  • Device Telemetry & Health: Real-time battery percentage, charging state, hardware GPS availability, and network connection type (5G/4G/WiFi) to ensure family members are aware of device connectivity status before power depletion.
  • Account & Authentication Information: Phone number (used exclusively for secure Firebase Phone Authentication & OTP verification), name, and optional profile avatar monogram.
  • Private Family Circle Data: Unique alphanumeric Family Group ID and encrypted member associations strictly tied to your private circle invite code.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To display live, high-precision location updates strictly to authenticated members of your private family circle.
  • To trigger automated arrival and departure push notifications when members enter or leave configured Geofence Safe Zones (e.g. Home, College, Workplace).
  • To deliver high-priority Emergency SOS alerts, alarms, and satellite coordinates to family contacts during distress triggers.
  • To maintain an optional, self-pruning 24–48 hour timeline of location points accessible only to your verified family.

3 Background Location Disclosure & Permissions

HomePro requests Android ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION permissions strictly for core safety features:

  • Continuous Transit Safety: Keeping parents and trusted contacts informed during late commutes or night travel without requiring the user to keep the app open.
  • Automatic Safe Zone Detection: Detecting arrival at destination in the background with zero user intervention.
  • Emergency Distress Reachability: Ensuring the device can be located immediately during critical SOS scenarios.
We NEVER sell, monetize, rent, or transfer your location records to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4 Data Storage, Encryption & Security

All communications between the mobile application and our backend are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 / SSL encryption. Data is hosted securely on Google Firebase Cloud infrastructure (Firestore & Firebase Realtime Database) with strict security rules guaranteeing that data is accessible exclusively to users verified under the exact same Family ID.

5 User Control & Data Deletion

  • Leave Family / Revoke Sharing: You can pause location sharing, turn off background updates, or leave a family circle at any time directly in the app settings.
  • Automatic Data Pruning: Historical location records are automatically and permanently deleted after 48 hours.
  • Full Account Deletion: You may request complete deletion of your account, phone record, and associated history by contacting us directly.
